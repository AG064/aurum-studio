Relay Yard: Night Shift

Active models, detail textures and shaders: original Sodium Enamel procedural kit,
provided with complete Godot authoring source under the repository MIT license.
See tools/asset_forge.gd, tools/forge_mesher.gd and assets/models_yard/manifest.json.

Legacy reference models: Kenney Space Kit and Space Station Kit, Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
https://kenney.nl/assets/space-kit
https://kenney.nl/assets/space-station-kit
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Selected models are optimized and grounded by tools/prepare_assets.mjs.
Their source names, vertex counts and SHA256 hashes are in assets/models/manifest.json.
